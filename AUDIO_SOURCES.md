# Audio sources

The Mavi 52 engine states use four recordings of the same Yanmar boat engine, recorded during a voyage near Vis, Croatia. Files were downloaded from Freesound's MP3 previews and are released under CC0 1.0.

| In-app state | Source recording | Author and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idle | `public/audio/mavi52-engine-idle.mp3` — “Yanmar_Engine_Slow.mp3” | [ivolipa, Freesound #325301](https://freesound.org/people/ivolipa/sounds/325301/) |
| Low | `public/audio/mavi52-engine-low.mp3` — “Yanmar_Engine_Closer.mp3” | [ivolipa, Freesound #325299](https://freesound.org/people/ivolipa/sounds/325299/) |
| Mid | `public/audio/mavi52-engine-mid.mp3` — “Yanmar_Engine_Fast_2.mp3” | [ivolipa, Freesound #325297](https://freesound.org/people/ivolipa/sounds/325297/) |
| High | `public/audio/mavi52-engine-high.mp3` — “Yanmar_Engine_Fast.mp3” | [ivolipa, Freesound #325298](https://freesound.org/people/ivolipa/sounds/325298/) |

The original file “Closer” refers to the recording perspective; it is used as an intermediate blend layer. Freesound marks all four recordings as [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The Kıyı 28 outboard engine uses six short loops cut from a field recording of a Yamaha 20 hp outboard starting, accelerating, running fast, and stopping. The loops correspond to progressively higher engine states. The source is published under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); attribution is retained here.
